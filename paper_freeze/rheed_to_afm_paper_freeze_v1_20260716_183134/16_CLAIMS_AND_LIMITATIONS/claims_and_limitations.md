# Claims And Limitations

## Can claim
- explicit sample-ID audit
- removelist enforcement
- strict growth-group OOF
- Rq surrogate prediction
- representative AFM retrieval
- no held-out AFM source in strict visual benchmark
- physical-height descriptors
- full provenance
- frozen model for future unseen prediction

## Cannot claim
- exact local AFM reconstruction
- pixel-level AFM accuracy
- unique morphology prediction
- independent external validation
- prospective accuracy before new AFM reveal
- completed closed loop
- full-cohort training fit as test performance
- oracle output as deployable prediction
- reviewer-rated realism before blind review scoring
