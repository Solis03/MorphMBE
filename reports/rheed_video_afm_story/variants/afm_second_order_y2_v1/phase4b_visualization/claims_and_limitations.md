# Claims and Limitations

## Can claim
- Strict group-held-out evaluation.
- RHEED-conditioned representative AFM retrieval/synthesis.
- Predicted-Rq-scaled representative morphology with provenance.

## Cannot claim
- Exact local AFM reconstruction.
- Pixel-level AFM reconstruction.
- Robust monotonic spotty-to-Rq relation.
- Neural generative-model superiority.

## Needs prospective validation
- Expert plausibility review.
- Prospective closed-loop predictions.
- Larger cohort calibration.
